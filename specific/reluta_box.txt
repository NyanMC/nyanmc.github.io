The Lunyar System Reluta Cardboard Box Sanitization Guidelines

tl;dr: Most yamls submitted to our asyncs will be reused in the Reluta async. We modify the yamls before sending them over,
including renaming slots to remove player names. This process is done by us, you don't have to do anything special. Yamls
for AD/unofficial games are not included. If you would rather not have your yaml included, please inform us, preferably
through a comment at the top of your yaml or the yaml description.



When one of our asyncs concludes, all submitted yamls are submitted to Elire, Astra, Vei for use in their Reluta async.
Yamls are altered using the following guidelines in order to maintain anonymity and fairness. This only affects Reluta,
and does not affect the slot played in the original async in any way, as this happens long after generation.
Nothing extra is required from the yaml submitter, this is done by us.

1. All games which are not allowed in AP main are deleted, such as those limited to After Dark or the Unofficial server.
2. Any games which have been /released due to issues or inactivity are deleted.
3. If any users express a request not to have their yaml submitted to Reluta, their yamls will also be deleted.
4. Games submitted by the same person are recombined into a single yaml, as multiworld.gg forcefully splits them apart.
5. All references to multiworld.gg (description mentioning MWGG, required version of 0.7.x, allow_collecting_from) are removed.
6. All slot names are renamed, typically following a format of game-yamlnumber. The yaml is renamed to sanitized-yamlnumber.
7. Game-specific steps are followed for applicable games (see below).
8. If admin cheats were used on a game (usually /send or /send_location), the yaml may receive changes to disable problematic options or exclude locations.
9. All yamls are packaged into a zip titled LunyarSystem(identifier)Yamls-sanitized.zip and uploaded to #cardboard-box-turn-in-flap in the Reluta Archipelago Side Server.

Game-specific steps:

Celeste (Open World):
- If golden strawberries have been enabled with an amnesty higher than 1, golden strawberries will be disabled.
- If the logic difficulty was set to assist mode, golden strawberries will be disabled.
- If the goal was set to poetry slam, it is changed to the first enabled goal level in the priority order of Farewell > 8C > 7C > 8B > 7B > Empty Space > 8A > 7A.
- Goal checkpointsanity will be turned on if checkpointsanity is enabled and the goal was changed due to poetry slam.
- Lock goal level will be turned off if the goal was changed (due to poetry slam) to one with a high number of locations (particularly 7A, or farewell with binosanity).
Rhythm Heaven Advance:
- Exempt from step 1 due to the following steps.
- The "patches" option is removed.
- The game name is changed to Rhythm Tengoku.
